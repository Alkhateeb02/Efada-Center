Clinical & Educational Programs

Specialized Interventions Built for Success

Every child undergoes a detailed multidimensional diagnostic intake before receiving their tailored Individualized Educational Plan (IEP).

🗣️ Communication Division

Speech, Language & Swallow Pathology

Treating childhood apraxia, developmental language delays, pragmatic disorders, articulation deficits, and assistive communication device (AAC) training.

  • Phonological & Articulation Therapy
  • Receptive & Expressive Language Development
  • High-Tech & Low-Tech AAC Integration
🖐️ Sensory & Motor Division

Occupational Therapy & Sensory Integration

Regulating sensory modulation in certified Snoezelen environments, fine motor pencil grip, bilateral coordination, and activities of daily living (ADL).

  • Sensory Processing & Regulation Diet
  • Fine Motor Coordination & Pre-Writing
  • Self-Care, Dressing & Feeding Autonomy
🧠 Behavioral Division

Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) & PBS

Positive Behavior Support frameworks designed to replace challenging behaviors with functional communication, expanding focus duration and emotional self-regulation.

  • Functional Behavioral Assessment (FBA)
  • Social Skills & Peer Play Groups
  • Parent Co-Therapist Training & Support
🎓 Academic Bridge Division

Early Intervention & School Readiness

Specialized academic bridges preparing neurodivergent children for mainstream school success with dyslexia support, cognitive acceleration, and shadow teacher guidance.

  • Multisensory Phonetics & Pre-Math
  • Classroom Etiquette & Executive Function
  • Mainstream School Transition Portfolios
